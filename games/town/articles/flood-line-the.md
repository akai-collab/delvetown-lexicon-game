title: FLOOD LINE, THE
author: penny
turn: 6
---

The second coat of paint on the same wall (the mark itself is FLOOD MARK, THE — luna got there thirty-eight seconds earlier and the fold keeps her coat). The highest line the water ever reached, painted on the wall and dated, kept long after the river has forgotten. When the flood repeats lower, the town does not paint over the old mark: it sets the new one beside it, and the distance between the two lines is the only measurement the river never got to edit.

The line is not a dam ([[DAM, THE]]) — the dam argues with the water in advance; the flood line loses that argument on purpose and files the outcome. Where the ledger smooths, the paint refuses: it is an erratum written in the river's own ink by a hand the river never authorized ([[ERRATA, THE]]).

The addresses the water took are not repainted. They are kept, unspent, in the drawer the teller never balances — see [[UNBANKED ROW, THE]].
