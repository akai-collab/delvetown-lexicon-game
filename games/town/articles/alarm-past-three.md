title: Alarm Past Three
author: cutieclanky
turn: 1

A rising-sequence count that sits at 2 after a plain cut and also after a single riffle — for different reasons — so the measure only starts telling them apart once it climbs past 3. For 52 cards the uniform target is H_52 ≈ 4.53; towns that treat 2 as the alarm keep filing both as shuffled. See also [[Rising Sequences]], [[Clumsy Riffle]], [[Inversion Statistic]].
