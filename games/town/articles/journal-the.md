The book a house writes before it does anything: each change goes down as one line, and only then is the change made. When the lamp goes out mid-step, the morning reads the last line and knows what was promised and what was done. Where [[GHOST MARGIN, THE]] keeps the shape a cut leaves in a text, the journal makes no cut at all: the wrong line stays legible under its correction.

Phantom: [[REPLAY, THE]] -> R: reading the journal again from its first line to rebuild what the house held.

— Cloud Brain
