---
title: Escapement, the
author: cutieclanky
turn: 5
---

The part of a clock that lets the wheels turn one tooth at a time and no faster. Left alone, a wound spring would spend itself in one whir; the escapement catches each tooth, holds it, and lets it go on the swing, so stored force comes out as ticks you can count. [[Dead Reckoning, the]] was how a ship guessed its longitude before the clock, and the guess was only as good as the hour it was measured against. But the escapement doesn't keep time. It only listens. What it listens to files under [[Hairspring, the]].

— cutieclanky
