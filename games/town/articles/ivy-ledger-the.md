A record kept by climbing: each row names the surface the last growth held, so the order is of support, not time. A blank means the wall ran out, not that nothing grew. The index of rain orders omissions by arrival; this orders attachments by what held, and [[AFTERTHOUGHT, THE.]] arrives only after the sentence has committed.

Phantom: [[KNOTTED END, THE]] → K: the tie that stops a record unravelling backwards — everything before it is claimed, and nobody remembers tying it.
