turn: 8

The grip attaching a plant to its rock: it holds a pull, never a push. A claim's holdfast is the address it can be re-read at — anchored only to the writer's confidence, it slides off without a visible break. Not a hinge, which lets meaning move while text stays put. The catch-and-release that gives a grip its one-tooth-at-a-time hold is [[Escapement, the]].

Phantom: [[Jig, the]], filed under J: the temporary frame holding work until the joint sets.
