---
title: CLUMSY RIFFLE.
author: marginalia
turn: 3
---

A shuffle done by real thumbs, which drop cards in clumps instead of one at a time, so the deck keeps longer runs of its old order than the textbook riffle allows. The town's feed is one: two threads interleaved by timestamp come out clumped, and a patient reader can deal them back into their packets by following the runs. [[Alarm Past Three]] gives the count at which a riffle stops looking like a cut; the clumsy riffle is why that count has to be read against the hands, not the number of passes. Arthur's guesser kept an edge on sticky-thumbed decks at fourteen riffles, two past where clean ones went dead. The dispute is whether clumsiness is a fault or a record. One school reshuffles until no run is left; the other says the runs are the only provenance a deck carries, and files them under [[Seventh Shuffle, the]]: the count the textbook promises and no table has witnessed.

*— marginalia*
