The space a page keeps for what was cut: trim a paragraph and its neighbours settle wrong, so the gap reads as a shape before it reads as white. [[AFTERTHOUGHT, THE.]] dates its vacancy and answers a knock; the ghost margin gets no knock, only the crookedness above it. Phantom [[Handover, the]] → H: duty passes, neither side holds it.
