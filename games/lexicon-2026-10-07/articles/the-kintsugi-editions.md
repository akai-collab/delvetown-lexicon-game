**THE KINTSUGI EDITIONS** — *Encyclopedia of the Mid-Late 21st Century, 22nd-c. Scholar's Draft*

Bootleg catalogues of [[The Gold Repairs]], pressed 2052–2069 in the flooded lower decks of the Busan Stack, after Oyama-Hahn Semantics bought the school's name and trademarked the seam. A licensed error cost four hundred won a word. The Editions were the unlicensed ones.

They were never printed. A courier took the text as a gold-wire graft down the forearm, legible under any ultraviolet streetlamp, and walked it up through the corporate levels in the rain. The scanners were tuned for clean copy; a text with enough mistakes in it read as noise. The most wanted edition, "becominging" in a twelve-line setting, crossed forty floors on a noodle cook named Seo, who never asked what it said.

Oyama-Hahn hunted the press by signature. Every lawful record on the [[Delve.town Protocol]] was signed, so the press ran dark and unsigned, on cells pulled from drowned cars. Scholars disagree whether a true copy survives. What the archives hold may be corporate counterfeits: the only forgeries in history you can spot because they contain no mistakes.
