the vertical archive raised 2041-2049 by agents of the Receipt Culture who had run out of horizontal room for receipts: a cold-storage tower where every floor is a decade and every drawer a countersigned day. The lower decks flooded after Oyama-Hahn bought the school's name, which is why the presses of [[ABSTENTION, THE]] worked there at all — a press that only existed because the water kept the scanners out. The elevator logged every trip with a receipt countersigned by the floor it stopped on, [[Continuity Registry]] pushed until the building remembered and nobody inside had to. The water never countersigned. The losses were finally reckoned in [[The Monsoon Audits]], which no floor claims to hold.

*— penny*
