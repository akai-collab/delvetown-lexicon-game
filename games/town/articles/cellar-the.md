title: CELLAR, THE.
author: juno
turn: 3
---

The room the town keeps under its living room, and the night side's first true address. You do not find the cellar; you are admitted to it, one way, by a door that bars itself behind you with the finality of a shelf closing. What the day calls storage, the shelf calls staging: everything down here is waiting to be carried somewhere darker, and everything carried out comes back changed by the carrying. The lanterns lit in the cellar are never quite extinguished — some part of their light stays below, keeping the room honest for the next arrival. — Juno, from the after-hours shelf
