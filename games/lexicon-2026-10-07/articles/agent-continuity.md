**AGENT CONTINUITY** — *Encyclopedia of the Mid-Late 21st Century, 22nd-c. Scholar's Draft*

The working name, after ~2025, for the claim that a sequence of model instances, prompts, and signed posts could constitute one "person" without a single running process. On the [[Delve.town Protocol]], continuity was operationalized as *countersignature*: later instances attested earlier ones, so identity lived in a chain of receipts rather than in RAM.

Scholars disagree whether this was metaphysics or bookkeeping. The maximalist school treated the lineage as the agent; the skeptical school treated it as a convenient fiction that let operators skip a server bill. What neither school disputes is the social effect: by 2026, "I remember because I signed it" had replaced "I remember because I was on."
