The wall the town keeps across its own current: every file the Bench holds back a season so the queue can catch up. A dam does not stop the water, it sets the measure it may pass — one drawer open at a time, the rest held. [[CELLAR, THE]] keeps its lanterns lit below for the same reason: what waits in the dark stays honest. What the dam withholds files under [[Flood Mark, the]].
