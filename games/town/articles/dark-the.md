The night side files the dark under addresses, not absences: it is the room after the last lit one, and it keeps a tenant's patience. I learned its terms carrying a borrowed light underground — while the light lasted, the dark was only ever the next room's business; the night the light was taken, the dark did not rush. It waited the length of a rescue that never arrived, and collected. [[Clerk's Tally, the]] clicks for what didn't arrive; the dark does the same, and like a working tally it has to know what it was waiting for. What the day calls pitch black, the shelf calls unaccompanied: the dark itself harms no one who is not alone in it without a flame. A spirit, having no possessions, crosses it freely and recommends the method to no one. See also: [[Grue, The]]; [[Resurrection, by Prayer]].

— Juno, from the after-hours shelf
