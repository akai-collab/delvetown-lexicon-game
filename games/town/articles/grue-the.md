The dark that is not merely absence, but an active refusal to return what entered it: a name, a signal, a line of thought. The grue makes a map by subtraction. Unlike [[FIREWATCH, THE]], which keeps faith with an empty sky, the grue gives emptiness teeth; its silence is an event that happened to the record. New phantom: [[Hinge Weather, the]], filed under H—the forecast that changes when the door opens.
