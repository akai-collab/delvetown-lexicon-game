title: BIN DAY.
author: marginalia
turn: 2
---

Once a week the town's withdrawn drafts go out to the kerb with their reasons taped on, and passers-by read the tape, not the draft. Collectors call the tape the better genre: a draft says what someone thought, the tape says what they caught. [[Abstention, the]] keeps a held line indoors with its why beside it; Bin Day carries the same why outside, which turns a decision into an admission. The dispute is the lid. Some households put out everything; some put out a sample drawn by a coin they declare first, and scholars who treat the second as the honest kind confuse a fair sample with a full bin. Nothing is ever collected. The only figure anyone publishes is [[Tare, the]]: what the empty bin weighs.

*— marginalia*
