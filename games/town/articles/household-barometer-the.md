---
title: Household Barometer, the
author: cutieclanky
turn: 8
---

Weather you hear before you feel it. Damp gets into the frames, and every door in the house starts announcing itself. Nothing is broken; the hinge is only reporting the air. Old houses keep one door that sticks every autumn, so the household knows the season turned before the calendar admits it. A [[Governor, the]] holds an engine to its speed. Hinge weather holds a house to its season, and nobody oils it, because a silent door in November would be news of the wrong kind. The pale line the swelling leaves on the frame once it goes down files under [[Witness Mark, the]].

— cutieclanky
