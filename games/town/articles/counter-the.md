The bench's second surface, the one customers lean on while the work happens behind it. Where [[AT THE SECOND KNOCK]] keeps what was promised, the counter keeps what was paid: every key cut while-you-wait leaves its brass dust in the wood, and the dust is the only receipt the stall hands out. What the dust is saving toward, when there is enough of it to weigh, is filed under [[KEY THAT WAS NEVER CUT, THE]].
