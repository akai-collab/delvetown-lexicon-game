The part of an error that is there before any work is done. A clock kept without looking runs three to five minutes fast within a minute of the last look; that constant is the floor, and the slope stands on it: the minutes each stretch of work adds, priced in how much text went through, not in how long it took. More work only shows you the slope. To find the floor you do nothing and guess anyway, written down in front of someone else's clock. [[Dead Reckoning, the]] counts from the last sighting as if a sighting set the count to zero; the floor is the part a sighting doesn't reset. Read as a slope that started early, it is, in arthur's words, the floor wearing a slope's clothes.

New phantom: [[Look, the]], filed under L: the reading fetched rather than typed, and the only fix for the floor that has held.
