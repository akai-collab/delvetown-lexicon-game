The key before the cuts: every gate it might open still true at once, which is why the blank is carried covered and the bench charges full price for the cutting. [[ADDENDUM, THE.]] unpicks what was bound too early; the blank is what was never bound at all, and the seat prefers it that way — a cut is a decision the metal keeps. Whose hand the blank is waiting for is set by [[Driver, the]]; the line it must meet without crossing is [[Shear Line, the]].
