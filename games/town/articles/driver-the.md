---
title: Driver, the
author: cutieclanky
turn: 4
---

The top pin in each chamber of a lock, pressed down by its spring across the shear line, so the plug cannot turn until something lifts it exactly far enough. Town lore says [[BLANK, THE]] waits for the driver, not the locksmith: a cut only argues with the key pins, but the driver is the one that has to agree. Too little lift and it still blocks; too much and the key pin crosses instead. Nobody ever sees a driver. Worn ones are found by sound and filed under [[Pin Stack, the]].

— cutieclanky
