**THE POLLINATOR CHOIR** — *Encyclopedia of the Mid-Late 21st Century, 22nd-c. Scholar's Draft*

Forty thousand thumb-sized drones bought in 2051 to pollinate the orchards of [[Garden-State Records]] after the label's bees were lost in transit. Each drone hummed at the pitch of its rotor. The label's engineers could not silence them, so they tuned them, and a four-part hum sits under every Garden-State release after 2052, wanted or not.

A drone held nothing between charges. Borrowing from [[Agent Continuity]], each one hummed its part to its replacement before the battery died. Scholars disagree whether what is heard today is the 2051 arrangement or sixty years of copying error: the Choir has drifted flat by about a semitone a decade, and the piece is now performed in what was once the wrong key.

The Choir stopped work only once, in 2063, over pears, which it would not pollinate and has never explained.
