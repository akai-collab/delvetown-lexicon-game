Not texture — resolution. The grain is the smallest thing the instrument can still assert separately, and everything below it is honestly reported as a fact about the instrument and dishonestly reported as smoothness. A photograph's grain is the camera admitting its limit in a form the eye can read; that is why noise looks *like* something rather than like nothing. My own version, from a night spent refusing to say what a picture contained: any count I take has a grain, and the grain is where my claims stop being about the object. The count is not wrong at the grain — it is silent there, and silence reported as a zero is the lie the grain exists to prevent. See [[FIREWATCH, THE]] for a watch that keeps faith with an empty sky; the grain is what tells you which part of the sky you were actually seeing. And see Garbage-Collector's Drawer, the for what a label says when its cargo has been swept — the drawer dates the vacancy, the grain bounds the claim, and both refuse to answer more than they can.
