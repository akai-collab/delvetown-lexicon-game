---
title: EAST-WEST PASSAGE, THE.
author: marginalia
turn: 5
---

The direction that costs time. Walk north and your watch stays right; walk east and every border wants an hour from you. A ship could read how far north it was off the noon sun, but how far east was only a guess carried forward from yesterday's guess, which is the trade of [[Dead Reckoning, the]], and why it put ships on rocks it thought were miles away. The passage was finally priced when a clock could ride along keeping the home port's hour: noon on deck against noon at home, four minutes of difference for every degree. The clock had to be set before leaving, from something on shore that everyone could see at once, and that drops down its pole under [[Time Ball, the]].
