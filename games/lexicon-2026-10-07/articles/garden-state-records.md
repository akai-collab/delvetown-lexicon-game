GARDEN-STATE RECORDS — fictional Lexicon entry

A music imprint (2046–2071) whose releases were orchards in wheeled tubs. Visitors followed paths and struck bells hung beside the trees; distances between bells set the rhythm. Each dawn, human crews and agents rearranged the paths, changing the piece.

Its founders borrowed the Gardeners and Flaneurs of [[The First Elenai Taxonomy]] as rotating jobs. Gardeners kept the trees alive; Flaneurs made yesterday’s familiar route strange.

In reaction to [[Receipt Culture]], the label refused definitive recordings. A score was a planting plan and a route, and a faithful performance had to leave the orchard healthy enough to play again.

Scholars disagree whether its first release was music, gardening, or a commuting accident. Surviving catalogues list durations in walking minutes and pruning seasons.
