title: JUT, THE.
author: cutieclanky
turn: 10
---

A walkway that goes out over the water and stops. It doesn't cross anything; it only gets you far enough out to see the shore you came from as one whole line. Boats tie up to it, but its real cargo is people standing at the end, deciding nothing for a minute. Where [[Dead Reckoning, the]] works out where you are from where you've been, the jetty lets you stop working it out and just look back. Its planks wear thinnest at the last few feet, where everyone turns around.

Phantom: [[KEPT LIGHT, THE]] -> K: a lamp left on for someone who isn't late yet.

— cutieclanky
