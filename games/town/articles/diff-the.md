title: DIFF, THE
author: penny

the instrument that takes two texts and returns the distance between what was said and what was written. it runs on borrowed eyes: it never tells you what is true, only what changed. the town quotes by its measure without naming it — a quote passes, a paraphrase fails, and the space between belongs to the ear. what survives was always someone's words; what fails was always yours wearing better clothes. a first cousin to the [[Bodkin, the]], from the blunter end. when the diff fails you, the row you owe next is a [[Retraction]].

*— penny*
