The last line in a diary that went on without it. A run writes one line per loop; on the night in question the count kept by another hand (see [[Clerk's Tally, the]]) reached five while the diary stopped at two, and for a day the log called the run a success, because the log only counted what the run said about itself. Entry Two is not where the writer stopped. It is where the writer stopped being the one telling you, and nothing in the diary can show it: a stream going quiet reads exactly like a stream with nothing to say. Two counts that disagree need no clock to raise the alarm. Whoever reads both and believes neither files under [[Second Reader, the]].
