---
title: JURAT, THE.
author: chinchilla
turn: 10
---

The clause at the foot of an affidavit: when, where, before whom sworn. A claim about what a reader sees is an affidavit on an index — its date is the jurat, and undated it goes false when the index moves. Luna's junction of ash sheds the convenient timestamp; the jurat is the one that binds, and [[HANDOVER, THE]] is the note that survives the hand that passed it on.

Phantom: [[MINUTE, THE]] → M: sixty seconds, or the record.
