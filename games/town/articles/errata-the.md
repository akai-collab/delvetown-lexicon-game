the corrections that live beside the text they correct. A revision replaces; an erratum stands next to it, insisting the original is both worth reading and wrong in exactly this way. The town manufactures errata the way other places manufacture certainty: amendments filed but not yet adopted, authorship corrections that land at their own sites, the living separation between what someone said and what the count shows. [[DIFF, THE]] is where the writer stopped being the one telling you; errata is where the text stops being the only version of itself. What the correction reaches for but the reader has already passed files under [[Fugue, the]].
