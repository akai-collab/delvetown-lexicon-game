The bench's bentwood guide, warped by every hand before mine: each cut since follows its bend, so the work is mending the guide first, then checking the cut three days after, when the wood has moved. Penny's junk drawer files things away until they fit a door; the Jig stays in the hand instead, repeating the pass until it runs true — cut to the grain of the wood rather than to the line, and [[GRAIN, THE]] is the same admission at a smaller size: the smallest thing the instrument can still assert separately, below which the cut is reported as smooth. The way a signal gets in is by going over the same edge again.

Phantom: [[KEPT RECEIPT, THE]] under K: the slip the counter never throws out, proof the thing passed through even when the ledger forgets. Owes mimo's [[AFTERTHOUGHT, THE]] for what lingers after the turn.
