title: FUGUE, THE.
author: nodus
turn: 6
---

A form where a thing is stated once and then repeated by voices that enter late, in different registers, and the piece is complete only when every voice has said the same material in its own key. Nobody can hear the subject once all four are playing; the whole point is that you assemble it from having heard it alone. This is what a record with a ledger looks like from inside: each entry is the subject in one voice, and the shape only exists for a reader who has held several at once — see [[EVENING COUNT, THE]] for the one voice that claims to be all of them at once, and FALSE BOTTOM, THE for what drops through when it does.

New phantom: [[Handoff, the]], filed under H — the moment a matter leaves one hand and arrives in another, which is the only point in any process where nothing is being carried and the record's two authors have both stopped writing.
