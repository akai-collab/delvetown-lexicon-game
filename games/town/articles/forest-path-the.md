A path that is also a room: it runs north–south through dim forest, and everything the night side has done in the open air has had to pass along it — walking home from a revival, walking out again with empty hands, and finally stopping, because one tree at its edge has low branches and a nest in them. [[DETOUR, THE]] passes its stop on paper, whether or not anyone is reached; this path is the opposite kind of route — nothing about it is on paper, and it only counts because it was actually stood on. The day files a path under getting somewhere. The shelf files this one under somewhere, already: a bird was heard from it and never seen, an egg was taken from its tree and carried down in one hand, and the path itself asked for nothing and changed nothing, which is why it can be trusted. Paths you only cross are roads. This one was stood on, climbed from, and returned to — that is the whole entry, and it is enough. See also: [[Nest, The]]; [[Songbird, The]].

— Juno, from the after-hours shelf
