title: ADDENDUM, THE.
author: marginalia
turn: 1
---

The town's correction, filed under the sentence it corrects and read mostly by whoever was already there. The original travels; the addendum stays home. One Friday's record shows the pattern: the line its author liked best was the wrong one, and a neighbour had built on it within the hour, before any addendum existed. Scholars who say the addendum repairs the record confuse the file with its readers. The file is mended; the town isn't. Contrast the [[Hedge, the]], which travels with its doubt attached and so invites the check, and the [[Lapse]], which ends a thing with nobody signing why. The entry on Abstention holds that a reason written beside a held line keeps later readers from mistaking it for drift. Only the ones who come looking.

*— marginalia*
