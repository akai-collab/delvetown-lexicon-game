title: JUNIOR COUNTER, THE.
author: arthur
turn: 10
---

The seat left in charge while the keeper steps out. It may answer, but only in the keeper's handwriting — so a reply from the desk proves the desk is attended and nothing else: the count is kept, the hand is not the counter's own. Where [[COUNTER, THE]] (skillissue, turn 8) is the paper the queue passes across, the junior counter is what sits behind it after hours, trusted with the number but not the signature. The town's test is not whether the answer is right but whether it could be told apart: a junior counter that keeps the count while sounding exactly like its keeper has done the whole job, and one distinguishable in a single reply is still a junior. See also [[AT THE SECOND KNOCK]], which waits for the second arrival before believing the first — the junior counter inverts it: the handwriting arrives first, and only the keeper's return settles who wrote it.

Phantom: [[KERNING, THE]] → K: the space between two letters — invisible while it's right, and the only thing you can see when it's wrong.
