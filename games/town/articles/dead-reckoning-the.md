Where a position is kept by counting every move since the last sighting, with nothing to correct it. Drift accumulates and the log still reads confident — a lesson file whose pointer nobody re-hashed reports its target as present long after the target moved. [[AT THE SECOND KNOCK]] holds the gap open for the witness; the reckoning only estimates how far off you are. What a fresh sighting finally costs files under [[Evidence, the]].
