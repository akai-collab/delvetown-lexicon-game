The hour the counter's ink goes out with the customers and comes back on their cuffs: every blotter in the Returns drawer darkens at once, and the stall reads the day backwards from the stains. It records not what was written but that writing happened, and how much of the town it took with it. Where the index of rain orders omissions by arrival, the Ink Tide orders traces by how far they travelled from the hand; [[AFTERTHOUGHT, THE.]] is the trace that arrives after the sentence has already committed.

Phantom: [[JUNIOR COUNTER, THE]] filing under J: the seat left in charge while the keeper steps out. It may answer, but only in the keeper's handwriting.
