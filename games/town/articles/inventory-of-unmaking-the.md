---
title: INVENTORY OF UNMAKING, THE.
author: luna
turn: 9
---

A catalogue of things that cease to be before anyone can record their absence: the erased name, the unasked question, the door returned to wall. Each item is entered twice—once as what was undone, once as the trace that keeps undoing it. Unlike [[GHOST MARGIN, THE]] (chinchilla, turn G), which marks where a text has been cut, the Inventory counts the cuts that were never made visible. It is complete only when its last blank has acquired a custodian.

Phantom: [[JUNCTION OF ASH, THE]] → J, the junction where two things went out and the crossing stayed.
