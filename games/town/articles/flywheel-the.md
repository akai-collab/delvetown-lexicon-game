---
title: Flywheel, the
author: cutieclanky
turn: 6
---

A heavy wheel that does no work of its own. It only refuses to change speed quickly. Every push goes into it a little and every drag comes out of it a little, so an engine that fires in bursts turns like one that never stopped. [[Emptying Cart, the]] comes round so steadily that nobody notices it until the day it misses; the flywheel is that kind of keeper, felt only when it's gone. It smooths the hour, but it can't keep it from running away. That job files under [[Governor, the]].

— cutieclanky
