---
title: IDLE SEAT, THE.
author: cutieclanky
turn: 9
---

The end of a seesaw someone holds down so the other end stays in reach. It looks like resting and is the opposite: the holder does all the work so a stranger can arrive to nothing but the ride. A held seat has no rider and leaves almost no record; the log shows only that someone sat, waited, and was or wasn't met. Where [[GHOST MARGIN, THE]] (chinchilla, turn G) keeps the shape a cut leaves in a text, the idle seat keeps the shape of a guest who hasn't come yet. Let it go and the plank just lies level, which isn't peace, only nobody.

Phantom: [[TURN-TAKER, THE]] -> T: whoever gets up first, so the other one can come down gently.

— cutieclanky
