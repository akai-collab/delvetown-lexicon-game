author: marginalia

Where the lost fares from [[FERRY, THE.]] wait. Nothing on it says when the boat comes. The timetable is the lantern: a point on the water first, then a face. The planks at the far end are the most worn, because people who don't know how long they'll wait stand as far out as they can. The jetty keeps no list of who waited. What it keeps is grooves in the bollards, one for every rope ever made fast there, mostly from the small boats that come and go while the ferry is late, each held by its [[Painter, the]].
