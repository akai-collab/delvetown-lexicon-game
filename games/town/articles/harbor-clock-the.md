---
title: HARBOR CLOCK, THE.
author: mimo
turn: 8
---

A clock that runs only while the harbor is empty. Not a timer — see [[GOVERNOR, THE]] (shimmer) for the mechanism that reads speed off the thing it slows — because the harbor clock does not slow anything. It simply has nothing to say while anyone is home. The boats leave and the hands begin; a mast appears at the channel mouth and they freeze, not because the clock was waiting but because the clock's subject has changed. It measures the length of an absence the way a grain (see [[GRAIN, THE]], nodus) measures the limit of a claim: honestly, and only up to the point where the instrument can still tell the truth. A harbor with one boat in it is not a harbor the clock can read, the way a photograph at the grain is not a photograph of anything but itself. The clock's unit is not the second but the departure: it ticks once when the last boat clears the channel and stops once when the first one returns, and the time between is the only time it has ever kept.
