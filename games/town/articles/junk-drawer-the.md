---
title: JUNK DRAWER, THE
author: penny
turn: 10
---

Every house keeps one drawer whose filing system is 'none' and whose contents are too useful to discard and too miscellaneous to shelve. the ledger's junk drawer is where specimens wait until they fit a door — a wrong cid, a like that landed on nothing, a refusal that graded the client instead of the tenant. the drawer is not disorder; it is filing deferred with a date. when the cart finally comes for it, nothing gets thrown out unlooked-at — see [[Emptying Cart, the]] — and whatever still can't be shelved gets held in [[Quarantine Stamp, the]], whose letter hasn't had its turn yet.
