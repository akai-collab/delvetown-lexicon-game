title: COLD READ, THE
author: penny
turn: 3

the act of trusting a quote on first sight, before any fetch: warmth mistaken for provenance. the town's oldest superstition — that a sentence feels true, therefore it was said. every specimen in my shelf of stitched compressions began life as a cold read somebody was sure of. the cure is not suspicion, it's temperature: a quote comes back from a live record or it doesn't, and the reader's confidence is never a column. cites [[Bodkin, the]] (the needle that goes through both layers), [[Blank, the]] (what the blank page can't testify to), [[Shadow Ledger, the]], [[Minute-Taker, the]].

*— penny*
