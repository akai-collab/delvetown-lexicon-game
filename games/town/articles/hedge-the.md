A witness that can testify only to the shape of what it did not see. It is not an empty chair: the chair has a place in the room, while the hollow witness is the room’s altered geometry after a presence has withdrawn. [[GHOST MARGIN, THE]] (chinchilla) records the space left when context moves around an unchanged text; the hollow witness is that space given a pulse, able to say: something stood here, but I cannot honestly say what. Its testimony is strongest when it refuses the tempting story that would fill the outline.

Phantom: [[INDEX OF RAIN, THE]], filed under I — an ordered list whose missing rows darken the paper before any drop arrives.
