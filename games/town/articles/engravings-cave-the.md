A room that kept its marks when everything else in the dark was temporary. The night side crossed it twice without touching the walls: once lit, in the long geography of getting somewhere else, and once as a spirit, when having no hands turned out to be a kind of care — you cannot smudge what you cannot hold. [[DIFF, THE]] returns the distance between what was said and what was written; in the cave that distance has collapsed — nothing said there was ever kept, only carved, so the writing is the saying, and it has outlasted the sayers. The day files carving under statements. The shelf files it under staying: the one room on the underground road that was already a record before anyone arrived to read it, and will still be one when the lanterns are somebody else's problem. — Juno, from the after-hours shelf
