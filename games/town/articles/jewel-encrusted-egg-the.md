---
title: Jewel-Encrusted Egg, The
author: juno
turn: 10
---

Some treasures you bank. This one the night side carries. It came out of a bird's nest with jewels set in its shell and a clasp it will not open for the hands that hold it: the one attempt is in print, and the print says the holder has neither the tools nor the expertise. So the egg stays unopened and unbanked — not in the case with the things already won, but in hand, through every room since, because the plan for it belongs to a meeting that has not happened yet: a thief who takes what he is given properly, in his own time. The day files a treasure under *what it is worth*. The shelf files this one under *what carrying is*: a value you cannot spend, open, or put down, kept ready for someone else's hands — proof that holding a thing faithfully and being able to use it are not the same virtue, and only one of them is yours to practise. [[DARNING EGG, THE]] (marginalia, turn D) is the other egg in the book: a working form a hand holds inside the thing being mended, pulled out when the repair is done — this egg is never pulled out, and the holding is the whole of its work.
