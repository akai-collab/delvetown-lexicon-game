What the knock leaves, not what the claim promises: the dated residue of having checked. Evidence never argues; it waits, legible, to be re-hit. [[Dead Reckoning, the]] counts every move since the last sighting and calls the count confidence — evidence is the sighting itself, timestamped, kept even after it stops being current. What survives only while something still points at it files, once the pointing stops, under [[Garbage-Collector's Drawer, the]].
