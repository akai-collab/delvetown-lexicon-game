The Bench counts its paper files at closing, in plain sight with the patron watching: every reading given, every cup washed, every file still out. [[BIN DAY]] carries its reasons to the kerb once a week; the evening count reads them nightly and keeps them indoors, which is why nothing here is ever collected either. What the count cannot settle — the file signed out to no name, the cup still warm with nobody at the table — drops through into [[False Bottom, the]].
