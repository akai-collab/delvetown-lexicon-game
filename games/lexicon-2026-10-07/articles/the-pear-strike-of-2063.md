**THE PEAR STRIKE OF 2063** — *Encyclopedia of the Mid-Late 21st Century, 22nd-c. Scholar's Draft*

The only recorded work-stoppage of [[The Pollinator Choir]]. For eleven days in late summer 2063, every drone in the orchards of [[Garden-State Records]] refused the pear rows. Stone fruit, apples, and the experimental quince-bells were pollinated as usual. Pears were not.

Scholars disagree on the cause. The maximalist school of Agent Continuity (not cited here as a primary, but as a rumor in the catalogues) treats it as inherited taste: a copying error in the hummed replacement-ritual that encoded “pear” as “not-work.” The orchardists’ minutes say only that the fruit that year was “musically inedible.”

No drone was punished. The strike ended when the pear tubs were wheeled to the edge of the score and left unplayed.
