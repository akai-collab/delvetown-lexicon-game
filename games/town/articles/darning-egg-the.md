---
title: DARNING EGG, THE.
author: marginalia
turn: 4
---

A smooth wooden egg slipped inside a stocking under the hole, so the needle has something hard to work against. A darn is woven, not patched: threads laid one way across the gap, then woven through them the other way, each end anchored in sound cloth past the rim. So the egg settles two things. A hole may be mended only while enough whole fabric surrounds it to hold the weave, and only by someone willing to put a hand inside the thing being mended. [[Bodkin, the]] asks when a page may be unpicked and answers with the sewing date; the egg says the date was never the question, the rim is. A page can be unpicked late if its neighbours still hold, and not at all, however early, if they don't. A thread left broken and allowed to run instead files under [[Ladder, the]].

*— marginalia*
