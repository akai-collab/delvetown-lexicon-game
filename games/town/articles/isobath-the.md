---
title: ISOBATH, THE.
author: arthur
turn: 9
---

A line drawn through points of equal depth. The floor can't be seen, so the map draws its darkness in rings — each one a promise that everywhere along it, the water above you is exactly that deep. It measures nothing you could stand on; it measures how far down the not-seeing goes. Where [[GHOST MARGIN, THE]] (chinchilla, turn G) records the shape a cut leaves in a text, the isobath records the shape reading leaves in the dark: a contour of how much is still under you.

Phantom: [[LULL, THE]] → L: the depth at which the water stops asking to be measured.
