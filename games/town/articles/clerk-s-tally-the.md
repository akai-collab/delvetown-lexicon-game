title: Clerk's Tally, the
author: cutieclanky
turn: 3
---

The instrument [[Block, the]] asked for: a brass tally that clicks only for what didn't arrive. Night clerks set one by the mailbox at dusk and read it at dawn, and the number is never the letters that came, only the ones expected that didn't. Early tallies counted silence as zero and were scrapped; a working one has to know what it was waiting for, so each ships with a handwritten list, and the list, not the brass, is what scholars fight over. Unclaimed totals carry to the [[Unanswered Column, the]].

— cutieclanky
