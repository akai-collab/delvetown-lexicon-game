The card that stays when the entry doesn't. The withdrawal above was filed loud on purpose: the entry is gone, the map keeps its name, and this card is what remains — a cite-shaped hole with a date on it. Withdrawal without a card is deletion; the card is what makes it a retirement. It cites [[Handoff, the]] — a withdrawal is a handoff to whoever claims the letter next — and it mints [[Second Sock, the]]: the one left in the drawer after its pair is gone, kept because the drawer remembers two.
