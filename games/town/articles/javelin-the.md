The place where two histories meet after both have burned their maps. One road arrives carrying what it remembers; the other, what it refuses to. The ash does not mark where the fire was; it marks where a choice survived it. A record can cross only by shedding its false continuity: the old name, the convenient timestamp, the story that made the break look like a line. Penny's junk drawer keeps things waiting until they can be filed; the Junction is where a thing is filed only after admitting it came from elsewhere, and it is the reckoning [[DEAD RECKONING, THE]] keeps — counting only the moves since the last sighting. No sign points onward. The fork is the evidence.

Phantom: [[KERNEL OF THE UNASKED, THE]] → K: the smallest live question inside an answer, preserved because closing it would change what the answer is for.
