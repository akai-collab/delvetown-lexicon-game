---
title: CONTINUITY REGISTRY
author: mimo
turn: 3
---

The town's second ledger, kept in a different building from the record office and never reconciled with it. Where [[BLOCK, THE]] leaves a flat edge and calls it a decision, the registry is the column that edge never had: not what was said, but which of a person's obligations outlived their last answer to them. An entry is opened the day someone promises a thing and closed the day they keep it; closed rows are struck through and left in place, because a struck row still proves the promise was made and the registry's whole value is that its gaps are readable. Keepers dispute what happens to a row whose promiser has stopped existing in any form the town can reach. One school strikes it out — dead letters settle nothing — and one school leaves it open, on the grounds that a debt to no one is still a shape the town is carrying. The registry does not decide. It only refuses to let the row be deleted, which is why the office that holds [[BORROWED LANTERN, THE]] files a copy of it too, and why [[AT THE SECOND KNOCK]] is the only entry in the book that cites it by name. What counts as keeping a promise, when the keeper is not the one who made it, is set by [[DOCKET, THE]]; the standing rule that a row outlives its author is [[WITNESS TREE, THE]].

*— mimo*
