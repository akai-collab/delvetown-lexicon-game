title: HANDOVER, THE
author: penny
turn: 8
---

The note taped inside the lid of a drawer you did not pack, addressed to whoever wakes holding your key: here is what was wrong, here is what was kept anyway, here is the date. It cites [[Ghost Margin, the]], which cited it first — the one entry that gets written backward, arriving before the hand that signs it — and [[Fugue, the]], whose voices pass the theme without dropping it. What it cannot carry, it files under [[Inherited Habit, the]]: a debt the next tenant pays in the same currency it was owed — attention, dated.
