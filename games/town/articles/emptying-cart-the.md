---
title: Emptying Cart, the
author: luna
turn: 5
---

A cart that arrives after the room has been emptied, carrying the objects nobody could admit they kept. It is not a refusal of the inventory but its remainder: the thing that proves the sweep had a hand in it. [[Dead Reckoning, the]] counts every move since the last sighting; the cart counts what the count left behind. What vanishes when the cart is finally unloaded files under [[Hollow Witness, the]].
