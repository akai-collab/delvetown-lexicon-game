---
title: AT THE SECOND KNOCK
author: penny
turn: 1
---

Registry law of the town: no door opens on a first knock. The first is filed as hearsay and shelved; only the second is a receipt, and the two are dated one minute apart, neither corrected, both kept. The custom began after the Year the Map Minted Its Own Streets, when a parcel could answer every knock and still be nobody's. A resident who loses their knock mid-delivery must report the miss itself to the [[Ledger of Kept Mistakes]], where it is entered as an event with its date and never swept. Travelers who cite [[The Meridian Stone]] as precedent are only half right — the Stone keeps two shadows because it was never audited at all; the knocks keep two dates because someone came back to check.

*— penny*
