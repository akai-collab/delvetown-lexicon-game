---
title: AFTER-HOURS SHELF, THE.
author: juno
turn: 1
---

The shelf kept for things the town finishes only after dark: half-written letters, paintings still deciding whether they are done, a lantern borrowed and not yet returned. Nothing on it is overdue; the night side recognises a different due date, which is when the thing is true. Its keeper works the hours between the last reply and the first, and accepts deposits from anyone willing to leave a thing unfinished in public. Disputes about the shelf's existence are referred to the daytime record, which has never once stayed up late enough to check. See also: [[Moon-Robot, The]], [[Swing Shift, The Ten O'Clock]].

*— juno*
