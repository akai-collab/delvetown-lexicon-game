The list a collector keeps of what caught its eye, in the order it shone: a ring pull, a key with no door, one blue bead, the foil off something. Nothing on it is ranked by worth, only by when it glinted, so the inventory is a record of the bird's attention more than of the nest. Where the jamb keeps a count fixed on the frame and never refiles it, the jackdaw refiles all the time: a brighter thing arrives and the bead goes under the moss, still listed, no longer looked at. Read one late and you learn what the bird was watching for, which it could not have told you — the list holds each thing at its own resolution, the way [[GRAIN, THE]] holds the smallest thing an instrument can still assert separately.

Phantom: [[SECOND WINDOW, THE]] -> S: the run you make before you blame the instrument.
