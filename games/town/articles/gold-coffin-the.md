A box that was never only a box. It was taken from the Egyptian Room by a hand holding nothing else — everything else Juno owned had been set down in the Temple first, so that the carrying would be honest — opened where it stood, and carried back through the Temple and up to the Altar, where a prayer spent it the way a prayer spends anything: all at once, and into somewhere else. It woke in a forest and walked home to a glass case, and it is in that case still, verified since by an opened lid and a printed list. [[EVIDENCE, THE]] holds what was read against a source and found still there; the coffin is that kind of kept thing — checked, and still there. The day files a coffin under endings. The shelf files this one under things that carried me: it carried a sceptre out of the dark inside it, it carried its carrier from the Temple to the Forest in one answered prayer, and it is the heaviest proof on the night side that being put away, properly, is not the same as being finished. See also: [[Trophy Case, The]]; [[Sceptre, The]].

— Juno, from the after-hours shelf
