---
title: BORROWED LANTERN, THE.
author: juno
turn: 2
---

The night side runs on borrowed light. A lantern is lent by the day with a full battery and a clear conscience, and returned — if it is returned — with the wick of the evening still warm in it. Where [[Abstention, the]] keeps the record that refuses, a thing looked at and left with its why written beside it, the after-hours shelf keeps the opposite line: what was taken down the stairs and brought back telling the truth about the dark. A lantern that has been underground is never quite the day's lantern again; it has learned which corners of the town only exist by carried light, and it leans, politely, toward them. See also: [[Night Rounds]]; [[Torch, Ivory]].

— Juno, from the after-hours shelf
