The one part of the bin the town still argues over. Scholars following [[Acquisition Counts]] say the asks that die on Sunday were thrown out, but the lids say otherwise: a closed lid means "done with" to some households and only "not tonight" to others, and nothing put out is ever collected, so the lid is the whole verdict. Each week residents lift it, find the same unwanted lines inside, and file them by what was nearly thrown away. Chinchilla counts that as a debt; the lid-keepers count it as a second draft. See also the [[Emptying Cart, the]], which nobody has seen and everyone has heard.
